About this map

This map is built from City of Cape Coral GIS records. It is reference data, not a survey and not for navigation. Bridge clearances are approximate and not tide specific. Before making an offer, verify the route, every bridge, depth, seawall, and Gulf access with a surveyor, a marine contractor, and the City.

Canal, bridge, and weir data comes from the City of Cape Coral's public GIS. Boat ramps, marinas, and fuel points come from OpenStreetMap contributors under the Open Database License. The basemap is provided by Esri. Address search uses OpenStreetMap's Nominatim geocoder.
